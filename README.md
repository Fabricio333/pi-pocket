# Vaca Inteligente Pocket

**vacainteligente.com** — a small Raspberry Pi companion for your room.

<img src="projects/vaca-pocket/assets/vaca-inteligente.svg" width="112" alt="Vaca Inteligente cow logo">

- Original cow logo, animated startup and emerald-and-cream device interface.
- Doorbell: a persistent Lightning address and 13 Argentine meme ringtones,
  with persistent shuffle/in-order rotation and Play all / Stop preview buttons.
- Speaker settings: volume, mute, hardware-button synchronization and Bluetooth
  scanning, pairing and output selection.
- Phone Wi-Fi setup: scan the hotspot QR, then scan the setup-page QR.
- Three-button app launcher, sound previews, clock and lighting controls.
